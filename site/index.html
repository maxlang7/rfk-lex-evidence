HR is a ratio of instantaneous death rates. RR compares risks. They are related, but not interchangeable. This plot compares uncertainty, not identical causal effects. 2017 paper · 2016 review

What does an interval mean?

An interval crossing 1 includes both lower and higher mortality. A wide interval means the estimate is imprecise. It does not mean that every value is equally plausible.

A confidence interval describes sampling uncertainty under the analysis assumptions. It does not account for every source of bias, and it is not a 95% probability statement about a fixed true effect.

How this was made

Evidence before allegiance.

This is an AI-assisted critical reading guide, prepared October 28, 2026. The video was downloaded locally and transcribed from its audio through OpenRouter. The scientific claims were researched separately. The transcript is an aid to listening, not an authoritative quotation record.

Assessments identify what is supported, what is contradicted and what remains unresolved. A single counterexample can refute “none.” A single observational study usually cannot establish causation. A null result cannot prove the absence of every harm.

This is a focused review, not a systematic literature search or a complete audit of every historical, political or personal claim. Review and trial funding, population differences, comparator choice and follow-up all deserve scrutiny. Clinical decisions require evidence specific to the product and person.
